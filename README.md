### Hi, I’m Ryan 👋

I’m an information security professional with 10+ years of experience across IT infrastructure, networking, security operations, security leadership, professional services, governance, risk and assurance.

My current focus is on **security assurance, GRC, technology risk and AI governance**, with a particular interest in how organisations turn security and regulatory requirements into practical controls, evidence, risk decisions and measurable assurance.

I started in infrastructure and networking before moving into security operations, leadership and advisory work. That background shapes how I approach governance and assurance: understanding how technology actually operates, assessing whether controls work in practice, and translating technical risk into clear business decisions.

### 🛡️ Security assurance, governance and risk

My recent work has focused on helping organisations understand risk, implement security controls and prepare for independent assurance and certification.

My experience includes:

- Information security governance and cyber risk
- ISO/IEC 27001 readiness, ISMS implementation and certification support
- Risk assessment, treatment and Statements of Applicability
- Internal audit and control effectiveness assessment
- Evidence assessment and audit readiness
- Remediation and corrective action management
- Third-party and supplier security risk
- PCI DSS assessment and advisory as a PCI QSA
- Security operations leadership and service improvement
- Senior stakeholder workshops, reporting and risk communication

I have supported multiple ISO/IEC 27001 programmes and delivered PCI DSS assurance across complex multi-site environments.

### 🔍 Controls, assurance and third-party risk

I’m particularly interested in the operational side of assurance: how requirements become controls, how evidence demonstrates effectiveness, and how organisations continuously understand whether their control environment is working.

Areas I’m continuing to develop include:

- Continuous controls monitoring
- GRC and compliance automation
- Third-party security assurance and TPRM
- Supplier criticality and risk tiering
- Inherent and residual risk assessment
- Continuous supplier monitoring
- Technology controls assurance
- Security metrics and management reporting
- M&A cybersecurity due diligence

I’m currently developing a portfolio case study covering **SaaS security governance, ISO/IEC 27001, compliance automation, third-party security assurance and AI risk**.

### 🤖 AI governance and emerging technology

I’m developing further capability in **AI Governance, AI Risk and AI Assurance**, building on my existing security governance and controls background.

My practical work has included:

- Reviewing AI-enabled governance and compliance technologies
- Considering AI use in ISO/IEC 27001 and PCI DSS environments
