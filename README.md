### Hi, I’m Ryan 👋

I’m an information security professional with 10+ years’ experience across IT infrastructure, networking, security operations, governance, risk and assurance. My current focus is OT security and the practical challenges of protecting industrial environments and critical national infrastructure.

I started in infrastructure and networking engineering before moving into security operations, leadership and advisory work. That background shapes how I approach security: understanding how systems operate, how controls work in practice, and how to communicate risk clearly to the business.

### 🏭 OT and energy sector experience

I spent more than two years embedded within the security team of a global oil and gas organisation operating in the UK energy/CNI sector, working across enterprise IT and OT environments.

My experience included:

- OT visibility and monitoring using Nozomi
- Security investigations and vulnerability management
- Identity security and secure remote access
- Security review of network, firewall, access and infrastructure changes
- Working with technical teams to assess risk and improve security controls

### 🛡️ Governance, risk and assurance

After moving into Security Operations leadership while continuing client advisory work, I transitioned into a dedicated Advisory Services Consultant role.

My work spans:

- Information security governance and cyber risk
- ISO/IEC 27001, ISMS, internal audit and control effectiveness
- PCI DSS assessment and advisory as a PCI QSA
- Third-party risk and security assurance
- Security operations leadership and service improvement
- Senior stakeholder engagement and risk communication

### 🔧 Technical foundations

My hands-on background helps me connect assurance requirements with how technology is configured, operated and maintained.

- **Infrastructure and networking:** Network security, firewalls, Linux and enterprise infrastructure
- **Cloud security:** AWS security, identity and monitoring services
- **Automation and scripting:** Python, Bash, Terraform and Ansible
- **Security tooling:** Splunk, vulnerability scanning, and application security testing tools
- **Development workflows:** Git, GitHub Actions, Docker and CI/CD security

### 🌱 What I’m developing

I’m continuing to build depth in **OT security**, including IEC 62443, industrial architecture and UK CNI requirements.

I’m also developing my **AI governance** capability. My work has included risk-based guidance for the secure adoption and governance of AI assistants and agentic tools, and I’m currently preparing for the **IAPP AIGP**.

I’m interested in practical security that brings engineering, operations and assurance together.
